# live-event-detection
Mini_project24
A software-based solution designed to enhance personal safety by detecting real-time threats through environmental audio analysis. This system targets individuals in vulnerable situations, such as those traveling alone or working in isolated areas. It uses a dataset of over 9,000 audio clips from 13 sound categories (e.g., gunshots, glass breaking) and employs advanced audio processing techniques like spectrograms and Mel-spectrograms to convert raw audio into visual representations that deep learning models can analyze. The system utilizes three deep learning models—1DCNN, 2D-CNN, and LSTM—which achieve high accuracy (90 above) in classifying threatening sounds. Integrated into a smartphone, the system continuously records environmental audio through the device’s microphone. If a potential threat is detected, the system automatically alerts registered contacts via email, SMS, or WhatsApp, including the recorded audio for verification. By using only the smartphone’s built-in capabilities, this approach eliminates the need for additional hardware, offering a convenient and cost-effective solution. This research highlights the potential of combining NLP and deep learning for real-time threat detection, providing a scalable and practical safety tool. The system could be expanded to include more sound categories and improved accuracy, further enhancing its usefulness in a wide range of real-world applications.
